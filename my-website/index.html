FOR MY WAVE 🌊

I was alone… sitting in the sand, feeling lonely in my life.

In that moment, you came into my life like a wave.
A wave that never stops…
again and again, you come to me, bringing so much love.

Even when I feel lost, you find me like the waves find the shore.
You never leave… you always return.

Thank you for being with me.
Stay in my life… like a wave that never stops.

LOVE YOU MY WAVE 🤍

- Ramya 🫂
